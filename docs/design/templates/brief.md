## Mode and lead
- **Mode:** Operate | Persuade — and why, in one sentence about who uses it and where.
- **Lead skill:** `impeccable` (Operate) | `design-taste-frontend` (Persuade).
- **Dials** (Persuade only): DESIGN_VARIANCE · MOTION_INTENSITY · VISUAL_DENSITY.

## The job
What a person comes here to do, the one thing they must never fail at, and
the device and setting (e.g. a phone at the door of an event).

## States
Every state the surface renders — empty, loading, error, each result, each
refusal. The design is not done until each has been designed, not just the
happy path.

## Constraints carried in
The CLAUDE.md invariants and DESIGN.md rules this surface touches, quoted by
name (e.g. "one `unmatched` outcome and one message for every miss").
Behaviour does not change in a design phase.

## Layout families
Which DESIGN.md §Layout families this page spends, one per section.

## Diverge (step 2)
The two `frontend-design` concepts, one paragraph each, and which the lead
adopted or rejected — with the reason. Receipt: `receipts/diverge.md`.

## Evidence (step 3)
Each `ui-ux-pro-max` lookup that informed a decision: query, the guideline
it returned, and the decision it supports. Receipt: `receipts/evidence.md`.
